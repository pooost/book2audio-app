Book2Audio -- turn a PDF/EPUB into a narrated audiobook (.m4b), fully on your
own computer. Nothing about your book or your voice ever leaves this machine.

GETTING STARTED

1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like (e.g. Desktop).
2. Windows: double-click Book2Audio.exe
   Mac: double-click Book2Audio.app
   (Mac may warn "unidentified developer" the first time -- right-click the
   app, choose Open, then Open again to confirm. You only need to do this once.)
3. Click "System / Diagnostics..." at the bottom, then "Download Missing
   Models...". This is a one-time download (a few GB) and needs internet.
   After it finishes, the app works completely offline.
4. Drop in a book, pick a voice, click "Convert to Audiobook".

NOTES

- The first conversion of a book is the slowest -- it needs to load the
  voice model. Later conversions (and resuming an interrupted one) are
  faster because completed work is cached.
- "Chatterbox" is the higher-quality, slower voice; "Kokoro" is faster and
  lighter but English-only. Pick whichever you prefer in Voice settings.
- If anything looks broken, open "System / Diagnostics..." first -- it'll
  tell you exactly what's missing.
